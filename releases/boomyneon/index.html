Boomy Neon

Kenn Loner

Produced, Mixed, and Mastered by: Kenn Loner
Release date: May 29, 2026

"Neon Vibe" is a vibrant Progressive House track that blends driving energy with lush, atmospheric textures. Following my previous releases as Kenn Loner, this track focuses on a "colorful" and melodic soundscape, designed to capture the pulse of a futuristic city night. I produced and mixed the entire track, emphasizing clean gain staging and a wide stereo image to ensure a professional club-ready feel. With its uplifting energy and pulsating neon-inspired synths, this track is perfect for workout, dance, or late-night driving playlists.
